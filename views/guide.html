guide

What everything on angeldomme is, and what it does to you. If this page and the site disagree, the site is right and this page is out of date. Say so and it will be fixed.

ACCOUNTS

Three ways in, and they are not the same thing.

discipleA full account. Holds points and angelcoins and can appear on the leaderboard. Invite only: you register, and Hermione decides. visitorA real account that sits outside the economy. Nothing pays out, but your progress and stats are still kept. guestNo account at all. A session under a temporary name, thrown away after three hours of silence. Offered under "no account?" on the way in.

Registration asks whether you are photosensitive. Answer honestly: yes turns off every flashing and strobing effect on your account. There is no way to change that answer afterwards yet, so if it needs changing, ask.

THE DASHBOARD

Where you land. The clock is US Eastern and counts down to the reset at 6am Eastern, which is when the wheel's turn and the check-in roll over.

  • Balance shows points, angelcoins and your rank.
  • Leaderboard lists whoever Hermione has chosen to list.
  • Games opens the wall. Everything is reached from there.
  • Settings holds the flashing-effects switch and the audio mixer.

POINTS AND ANGELCOINS

Two currencies, earned differently, not interchangeable.

Points are your standing with her. Signing in on a new day pays 5. Otherwise Hermione gives and takes them by hand, and that is the main way they move.

Angelcoins come out of the games. Snake pays 1 a pickup. The wheel pays 1 to 200 on its one turn a day, weighted hard to the low end: the mean is about 9.5, and a hundred or more turns up roughly once in eighty.

Visitors earn neither. Guests earn neither, and lose everything when the session ends.

RANKS

A ladder, lowest first: Servant, Follower, Devoted, Worshipper, Disciple, and two above those that you do not hold. Visitor sits off the ladder entirely. Everyone starts at Servant and only Hermione moves anyone. Click a rank wherever it appears to see the whole ladder.

THE GAMES

All of them are on the wall at /games. Deathroll, Chess and Elysium need an account; a guest can open the rest.

Snake

No grid: the head turns rather than snapping, and the body is the trail behind it. The food runs when you get close, and drifts on its own besides. Running into your own body ends the run, which only becomes possible once you are long enough to reach it.

Penance

Lines, for punishment. Take a preset or write your own, choose how many and how harsh. The screen corrupts as you slip, and on merciless one mistake sends you back to the start of the line.

Devotion

The same machine, calmer. Preset line-sets only, no difficulty, no corruption. Fifty lines completes the daily.

Wheel

One turn a day. The faces run past on a cylinder and whichever stops under the pin pays.

Deathroll

Two players and one falling die. Whatever you roll becomes the other player's maximum. Whoever rolls a 1 loses.

Chess

Against Hermione. You are white, she is black. She has powers you do not, and you will not be told what they are.

Elysium

A tree, kept in real time. It grows and sickens whether or not you are watching, so it is measured in days rather than minutes. Water, mist, trim, feed and inspect all have costs as well as uses. It starts damaged.

Dummy Parse

A damage sim against a target that does not fight back. Spend 100 stat points, learn a rotation, beat your own number. Every stat has diminishing returns, so stacking one is always worse than spreading. The timed 30 and 60 second runs are ranked; free runs are not, because you choose when they end.

Skill Check

A needle runs a dial. Press the key drawn in the middle while it is inside the red. Early, late and missed all fail, and the dial moves and changes size between attempts.

Summary

A real article and a word limit. Read it, then say it back inside the limit. Going over is the only way to fail. She reads what you hand in.

THE CONSOLE

Press ` on any signed-in page to slide a console down, and Esc to close it. Up and down walk back through what you have already typed.

Commands begin with pray. pray launch <game> opens a game by name, pray reload reloads the page, pray quit tries to close the tab. pray on its own is not a mistake.

Other things can be typed into it. Whether anything answers is not documented here.

WHAT THE SITE DOES TO THE SCREEN

Text flashes on its own, and the picture tears and distorts from time to time. Both are deliberate. Both are off entirely for anyone who answered yes to the photosensitivity question, and the flashing can also be switched off in Settings by anyone who would simply rather not.

Sound is mixed in Settings: a master level over music, ambience and interface noise, each with its own mute. A game with its own audio controls opens matching those, and changing it inside the game does not change these.

WHAT HERMIONE CAN DO

Approve and turn away registrations. Give, take and set points. Set anyone's rank. Choose who appears on the leaderboard. Delete accounts. Read every finished set of lines and every summary handed in. Write the Devotion and Penance presets, and the lines the login screen decrypts.

And whatever she has in the games that is not written down.
