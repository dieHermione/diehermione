Tending Elysium

A tree grows on its own, given time. Your task is not to grow it, but to keep the conditions right so it can keep growing.

The loop

  • Observe the tree and its surroundings. Soil, air, and leaves all tell you something.
  • Inspect when something feels off. A close look turns a vague worry into a clue, and a second look into near certainty.
  • Act with care. Each action has a cost as well as a use, so read the tree before reaching for a button.
  • Wait. Recovery and growth both take time. Patience is the real mechanic here.

Your hands

  • Water the soil, but only when it needs it. Soaked roots are their own kind of harm.
  • Mist raises humidity for the leaves. Wonderful when the air is dry, risky when it is already heavy.
  • Trim opens up the canopy and cuts away trouble, at the price of a fresh wound.
  • Fertilize feeds slow recovery. Never a cure, and easy to overdo.
  • Inspect reveals what the eye alone would miss.

What to watch for

Trees can fall ill. Trouble usually begins quietly in one part of the tree and, left alone, works its way to the rest. Watch for changes in colour and texture, and trust that anything can be recovered with the right care and enough time. The tree can never be lost.
